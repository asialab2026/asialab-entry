Meet Asia’s Official Crush: Rashmika Mandanna

Few stars shine as brightly—or as authentically—as Rashmika Mandanna. Crowned “Asia’s Official Crush” by her adoring fans, she has transcended borders, languages and industries, becoming a true cross-cultural icon. In this Aurora Exclusive, Rashmika sits down with us for a candid conversation about her rise to fame, her creative process, and how she stays grounded in the whirlwind of global attention.

On balancing fame with authenticity

“I think the most important thing is to never lose sight of who you are. Fame can be overwhelming, but at the end of the day, I’m still the same girl who loves long conversations, a good laugh, and comfort food.”

On breaking into the international stage

Rashmika’s work has spanned multiple industries, from South Indian blockbusters to Hindi cinema, and now collaborations that reach far beyond Asia. With upcoming projects that hint at a global crossover, she’s more determined than ever to represent her roots while embracing new opportunities.
