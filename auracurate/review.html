For the founder

Ten decisions only you can make

Each comes with the exact wording or choice, an alternative, and a recommendation from design.

01

Launch markets

Only a Korea checkout was seen. Shipping, duties and payment methods differ by country.

Co-CEO recommendation: Open physical shipping to Korea + two or three markets where Aurora’s audience already is (India, Singapore, the US) only after one test order each. Sell online programs and digital items worldwide from day one — no shipping risk.

02

The first complete flow

A narrow flow that works end to end beats a wide catalogue.

Co-CEO recommendation: Launch with K-Career Entry Session (live), one OHUI product with verified supply and Aurora Lounge (free). Add products and programs one by one on the same templates.

03

Community model

Tevello sells communities, cohorts, courses, workshops and digital items; the original screen had paid tiers.

Co-CEO recommendation: Free Lounge + paid programs at launch. Add a paid membership circle only when there is a monthly promise you can keep.

04

Host on Aurora (selling experiences)

Hosts propose; Aurora produces and sells. Shopify does not split payouts.

Co-CEO recommendation: Selective, contract-based revenue share settled by Aurora — not an open marketplace. Start with two or three hosts.

05

Fund and Together

Both need a payment rule: charge now with refunds, or charge only when the goal is met.

Co-CEO recommendation: Keep both as interest lists at launch. Open the first project only after one physical product has shipped reliably.

06

Hero line on Community

Current: “Where Global Voices Meet Influence”.

Alternative: “Where Global Voices Meet Culture”

Co-CEO recommendation: Use the alternative. “Influence” reads as something membership gives you.

07

Launchpad heading

Current: “Your Launchpad to Global Success”.

Alternative: “Your Launchpad to a Global Audience”

Co-CEO recommendation: Use the alternative — the value is the audience and the craft, not a promise of success.

08

Aurora 100 card

Current: “Help shape Aurora 100”.

Alternative: “Suggest someone for Aurora 100”

Co-CEO recommendation: Use the alternative so suggesting is never mistaken for applying or buying.

09

Prices, refunds and reply times

Program prices, refund terms, intake reply time and session scheduling are not set.

Co-CEO recommendation: Decide per program before it opens. The templates show “Price set at launch” until then.

10

Social links and image rights

Header and footer show no social icons until official URLs exist; photos need rights confirmed.

Co-CEO recommendation: Send the official Instagram and YouTube URLs, and allow cdn.shopify.com so product photos can be checked.

Codex handoff

What to build, and what to verify

Source

Code is the editable source: auracurate/. New in this delivery: cart.html, order.html, help.html, collection.html, offer.html, learn.html, application.html, project.html, ops.html, review.html; scripts assets/proto.js, commerce.js, learn.js, apply.js, people.js, ops.js; styles assets/journey.css; data content/offers.js. Figma was not used for this delivery.

Theme vs native

  • Aurora theme (build): all pages, cart, program pages, collections, help, Partner intake form.
  • Shopify native (reference only): Checkout, Thank-you and Order status, customer account sign-in. Branding via checkout settings; extra blocks only through extensions the plan supports.
  • Tevello (reference only): member home, courses, lessons, communities, posting, reporting. Confirm each capability before promising it; keep theme colours out unless its settings allow.
  • Email (wording only): order emails (Shopify notifications) and proposal replies.

Data contracts

One source per thing — no hand-copied data. Products and prices from Shopify; programs as Shopify products linked to Tevello courses; articles from the aurora-journal blog with aurora.display_areas; relations as product metafields. The component table is on the staff board.

Still to verify

  • One real order per launch market: payment, taxes, duties, delivery, refund.
  • Program purchase → Tevello enrolment → access email.
  • Restricted space direct-URL blocking.
  • Intake endpoint, inbox owner and reply time.
  • Product photos with cdn.shopify.com allowed (blocked in the design environment).

Assets needed

  • Original product photos for every live product (Shopify media).
  • Approved host and program images before any sample is replaced with a real offer.
  • Official social URLs.
  • Approved policy text for shipping, returns, privacy and terms.
