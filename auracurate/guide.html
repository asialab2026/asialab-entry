Official IP marks · Aurora 100 · Global Faces 100

Aurora 100 · Stage
Global Faces 100 · Stage
Aurora 100 · Read
Global Faces 100 · Read
Two standalone marksEach IP is its own brand: the name above the “100”. Aurora 100 uses the Aurora wordmark as its name; Global Faces 100 has no Aurora wordmark on top. Both share the same 100 from the Aurora 100 master.
The star is the oIn Gl✦bal the Aurora star replaces the lowercase o, exactly as it does in Aurora. Star height = x-height; never an ordinary o.
Type“Gl✦bal Faces” in Poppins Light, capitals only on G and F, tracking −1%, centred over the 100 at about four-fifths of its width. Soft glow on Stage, plain black on Read.
Meaning stays separateAurora 100 = established influence (authority). Global Faces 100 = discovery and possibility. Neither mark implies a rank, vote or paid place.

Use the master files only: assets/brand/aurora100_*.png and global-faces-100_*.png. White with glow on Stage, black on Read. Don’t redraw, recolour, stretch or add effects. Minimum width 120px on screen.

Content rules

Trust is the product.

Real or explicitA real, approved image — or an aurora empty state that says what is coming. Never a grey box.
No invented numbersNo views, followers, review counts, stock counters or “N people viewing”. Metrics only with a source and period.
No false urgencyNo countdowns, “almost gone”, “selling fast” or compare-at sale prices.
Ranking without numbersAurora 100 is an editorial selection. Ranking reads “Editorial selection”.
Samples are labelledOriginal design samples carry “Design sample”; cards without a real destination are not links.
Commerce is gatedBuy buttons only for products on sale in Shopify; prices from Shopify; checkout is Shopify’s.
Coverage ≠ endorsementFeaturing a person is editorial. It is not a Curator appointment, a collaboration or a product endorsement.
A request is not approvalPartner, Fund, Together and Made for You forms say so, and nothing is charged.
