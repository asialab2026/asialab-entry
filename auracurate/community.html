JENNIE × HERA — More Than Beauty, It’s An Attitude

Why HERA’s muse represents confidence, grace and the modern Korean spirit

There are brand campaigns you simply see on billboards. And then there’s HERA × JENNIE — the kind that lingers in your mind, not because of the glamour, but because it feels real. It’s not just makeup; it’s a reflection of how beauty has evolved in the heart of Seoul — bold, effortless and quietly powerful.

As the global ambassador of HERA, Jennie doesn’t just model the product — she embodies it. Every campaign, every photo, every gaze speaks of a woman who knows her worth. She isn’t selling perfection; she’s redefining it.

What makes this collaboration special

At first glance, it’s a typical luxury partnership — a high-end cosmetic brand and a global icon. But what happens when HERA’s philosophy of “modern elegance” meets Jennie’s signature confidence is something different. It’s not about surface-level beauty; it’s about how you feel when you wear it.

Jennie’s presence gives the brand a soul. Her calm yet captivating aura mirrors HERA’s vision — that beauty isn’t about standing out, but about standing strong. Whether she’s applying a new Rouge Holic shade or walking through Seoul’s neon-lit streets, she carries the message:

“You don’t need to shout to be seen.”
