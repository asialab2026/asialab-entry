03 · Together

Discover a release. Choose it together.

Some pieces only make sense in numbers — a limited edition, a set from a Korean maker, a curator’s capsule. Join the list; when enough people want it, the order window opens.

Join the list

See a Together page — simulated

  1. Show interestJoin the list for a release. No payment, no commitment.
  2. The terms are setPrice, minimum quantity, delivery and returns are confirmed with the maker.
  3. The window opensEveryone on the list is told first and orders through Shopify checkout.
  4. Made and deliveredA selected product with agreed delivery terms.
Together · Design studyAn eyewear edition

A curator’s frame, released when enough people choose it.

Together · BeautyK-beauty essentials set

A maker’s set offered at group terms.

What Together never does
  • No countdowns or “almost gone” pressure.
  • No charge until the terms are confirmed and you order.
  • No inflated numbers — we show only real list counts once a release opens.

Join the Together list

Tell us what you would choose together. When a release is confirmed, you get its price, minimum and delivery terms before anything is ordered.

04 · Made for You

Tell us what you want to make.

An object, an edition, a film, an experience or a personal archive — produced by Aurora with Asia Lab’s production team, for you, your brand or your community.

Open for briefs

See how a request works

  1. BriefTell us what, for whom and roughly when.
  2. Fit and rights reviewWe check whether Aurora is the right maker and what rights are needed.
  3. Scoped proposalScope, price, revisions and timeline in writing.
  4. Agreement and productionWork starts once you accept the project agreement.
  5. DeliveryYour piece, plus — if you wish — its story in Studio.

Made for You · IconsA personal archive, made with you

A made-to-order publication or digital edition.

Good to know
  • A brief is not an order; nothing is charged until an agreement is signed.
  • No celebrity participation is implied by any example.
  • Rights, scope, price, revisions, timing and cancellation are in the agreement.

Send a brief

A short brief is enough to start. If it is a fit, we reply with questions and a scoped proposal.
