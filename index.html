Core Deliverable

Direction Brief

Your Direction Brief is delivered within 48 hours of intake form completion. It contains your market direction, partner type identification, and immediate action items. One page. No padding.

Status
Awaiting intake form completion — Brief will appear here within 48 hours.
Brief Structure — What You Will Receive
01 · Market Direction
Which market to enter first, in what sequence, and why — based on your specific product, current stage, and competitive landscape. One or two markets, clearly ranked.
02 · Partner Type
The specific category of partner you need — distributor, influencer, local agency, co-production partner, licensee. Starter clients receive named individuals, not categories.
03 · Immediate Action
One to two specific actions you take in the next 7 days. Not a strategy document. A starting move.
04 · Next Steps (Starter only)
Three options for how to proceed. You select one. Asia Lab initiates connection based on your selection.
